# Changelog

## 2.0.1

- The floating and mini windows follow their width: drag them narrow or wide and the text, chips, buttons and coin resize to fit, and the jar stacks above the text when narrow.

## 2.0.0

A fresh start: this is the first published version. It includes:

- **Home**: total to transfer, money needed this month (from salary and from money saved for bills due), income with extra earnings paid this month, left in salary, where your income goes, the Expenses, Savings and Set aside boxes, Coming up month by month, and transfers to each account.
- **Bills**: monthly, quarterly, yearly and one-time entries. Yearly and quarterly bills are saved up a share each month and paid from what is saved in their due month.
- **Moonlights** (extra work): work types with pay per read, pay delay and TDS; work logged by day or by whole month; money coming in by pay month; a daily target from the monthly goal; streaks, badges, a month-end forecast; a quick Reads box on Home; and a floating window and a mini window with speed, an hourly log, read steps and a one-click coin.
- **Settings**: income and buffer, appearance and pop-up style, accounts, money at the start of the month, a calculator, export, and data and security.
- Everything is encrypted with your passcode before it is saved to your own private repository.
